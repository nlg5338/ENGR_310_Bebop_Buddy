How Bebop Buddy works

Bring any progression: a standard, a blues, or the tune your combo is rehearsing this week.

1

Enter your chords

Type the changes, like Dm7 | G7 | Cmaj7, or pick a built-in progression such as a 12-bar blues.

2

See what to play

Get the scale for every chord and the guide tones that make your lines sound like jazz, with a plain-English explanation of why.

3

Hear example solos

Bebop Buddy writes original solo lines over your changes, shown as sheet music you can play back and study.

4

Jam with a backing track

Loop piano, walking bass, and swing drums at any tempo, so you can practice like you're on the bandstand.

5

Level up with lessons

Follow a step-by-step path: chord tones, guide-tone lines, approach notes, enclosures, and bebop phrasing.

6

Save your songs

Keep your progressions, favorite solos, and lesson progress in your account and pick up where you left off.

Questions

Do I need to know music theory?

No. Buddy explains every concept in plain language as you go. Knowing how to read basic chord symbols helps, and the first lesson covers it.

What instruments does it work for?

Any of them: sax, trumpet, trombone, piano, guitar, bass, voice, and more. Solos are shown in standard notation you can read on your instrument.

Can I use the songs I'm learning?

Yes. Type in the chord changes of any tune and Bebop Buddy builds analysis, solos, and a backing track around them.

Are the example solos copied from real recordings?

No. Every solo is newly written by AI for your progression, so you get fresh material to study every time.

Can I cancel Pro?

Anytime, in one click from your account page. You keep Pro until the end of your billing month.
